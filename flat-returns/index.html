Three things it does

No workflows to configure, no tiers to unlock. The parts of a returns app that stores actually use.

1

Customer portal

Shopper enters order number and email, picks the item, and chooses: return for refund, exchange for another variant, or store credit. Store credit is issued as Shopify native store credit on the customer account, so it spends at checkout with no gift-card hacks.

2

Label via Shopify Shipping

Return labels are bought through your existing Shopify Shipping account at your negotiated rates. Charge the customer, deduct it from the refund, or eat it. Your call, per rule.

3

Rules you set in 5 minutes

Return window, final-sale tags, restocking fee, who pays the label, auto-approve under a threshold. Exchanges without forcing a return first are included, not gated.
