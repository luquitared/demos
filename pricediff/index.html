How it works

Step 1

Email the sheet and your export

Attach the supplier price list as it came (xlsx, csv or pdf, merged headers and all) plus a Shopify products export.

Step 2

We match and diff

SKUs are matched to your variants, with barcode and title fallbacks. New price is MAP, or MSRP when there is no MAP. Cost is dealer cost. Discontinued items are flagged.

Step 3

Import the CSV

You get diff.csv to check, apply.csv in Shopify's import format with only the changed rows, and a short summary of what needs a human look.
