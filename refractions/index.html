What this is.

Most podcast aggregators show you what each show said. Refractions shows you what happens when you stack them — the moment where the same proper noun starts meaning five different things depending on the feed it landed in.

Each issue takes one entity from the catalog's entity_mentions table, finds the shows that talked about it, and isolates how each show framed it. The aggregation is the story.
