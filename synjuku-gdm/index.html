Motivation

Egocentric human-demonstration sample for robot-manipulation foundation-model training, assembled for the Google DeepMind Human Ego Data pilot. Collected and produced by Synjuku.

Composition

  • 600 episodes × 60 s = 10.0 h, 4 synchronized cameras per episode (head/ego, left wrist, right wrist, exocentric), 960×720 @ 29.97 fps, H.264. 61 GB.
  • 9,384 language annotations (subtask spans, median 3.2 s, one sentence each), 100% machine-verified; label coverage averages 86% of each clip — unverifiable or incorrect labels were removed rather than shipped.
  • Per-frame tracks, merged into each episode's data parquet (21 columns/frame): image-space hand pose (21 keypoints/hand), ego camera position + rotation (COLMAP SLAM, up-to-scale), exo upper-body pose (9 joints), left/right hand pose in 3D world coordinates, with per-track timestamps and validity flags.

Collection

Worker-worn 4-camera rigs operated by consented collectors performing task-card-scripted activities in real working venues (guesthouses, factories, recycling facility, workshops), plus in-the-wild workplace footage for logistics/industrial categories. May–June 2026 (471 episodes) and March 2026 (129 episodes). Episodes are 60 s windows selected from longer continuous sessions by annotation density. Task definitions come from a versioned task-card registry; every episode carries its task-card id and full source provenance.

Quality control

  • Signal layer: 2,400/2,400 streams checked (resolution, fps, duration, black/freeze, SHA-256 integrity).
  • Label layer: every annotation verified against footage by a video-grounded LLM judge calibrated on a 240-span human gold set (91% flag precision). 2,376 labels repaired via verified blind re-captioning (provenance-tagged), 810 unverifiable/irreparable labels removed. Final re-sample: 0 contradictions in 1,800 spans.
  • Tracking quality (mean detection): camera pose 96.0% · body 98.3% · L hand 95.4% · R hand 93.1%; medians ≈99.3%. Per-episode rates in meta/tracking_quality_per_episode.csv.
  • Versioned: annotation states snapshotted v1.0→v1.4 with every label change logged.

Known limitations (disclosed)

  • 960×720 delivery resolution — 1080p+ available on fresh collection
  • SLAM positions are up-to-scale (no absolute metric scale); not gated against sub-cm targets
  • 8/2,400 streams (exocentric bonus view) at 23.98 fps
  • Hand-track tail where hands exit the egocentric FOV (right hand lowest, 93.1% mean)
  • Scenes are task-card scenarios staged in real working venues, not 600 distinct venues
  • Segment lengths: 68% within 1–5 s, 30% between 5–15 s (tunable on request)
  • Collectors are consented; no bystander blur pass applied in this version

Access & use

Read-only scoped S3 credentials to s3://synjuku-data/synjuku-gdmr-sample/. LeRobot-v3-compatible layout; tracks are pre-merged per frame — no joins required. Intended uses: manipulation policy/representation learning, hand-trajectory prediction, subtask grounding. Showcase renders (wrist-trajectory overlay, 3D pose) available alongside. How the label layer works

  • Calibrated judge. A video-grounded LLM judge (native video, 3-vote escalation on uncertain cases) calibrated against a 240-span human-annotated gold set: 91% precision on flags; recall on subtle action errors is the known limitation, reflected in the accuracy bound.
  • Full coverage. The judge watched the clip behind every span (10,122 verified) — initial contradiction rate 24.0%, consistent with the automatic annotator's error profile.
  • Blind repair. Every contradicted span was re-captioned by an independent pass that watched the clip without seeing the original label; each new caption was verified like any other label. 2,376 labels repaired (provenance-tagged qc_repair_blind_caption); 130 irreparable spans removed.
  • No gray zone. 680 spans the judge could not confirm were removed rather than shipped.
  • Re-sample. A fresh stratified re-sample of the final annotations returned 0 contradictions in 1,800 spans.

Traceability

Annotation states are version-snapshotted (v1.0 → v1.4: 11,313 → 10,197 → 10,067 → 9,387 → 9,384 spans) with every individual label change logged; each episode carries full provenance (source dataset, source episode id, window offset, capture site, task-card id).
