Long tail by design

175 task cards across 141 environment×site scenes at 10 real venues — hospitality, retail, logistics, industrial, home, office — capped at ≤3.6 h per scene so no single environment dominates. The delivered mix landed within ±5% of the lab's pilot targets in every category.
