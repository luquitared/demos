Reach the exit. Tap an adjacent cell, swipe, or use the pad (arrows/WASD too).

You move 1 step; mummies move 2×, the scorpion 1× — all chasing on the same priority axis (it's fixed per maze). Lure two monsters together to destroy them. Traps kill only you; a key toggles its gate.
